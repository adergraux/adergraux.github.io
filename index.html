Materials

Drag a material onto the grid, or tap it to pick it up and tap slots to place it (it stays in your hand). Tap a filled slot to pick it back up. Right-click clears a slot, Esc empties your hand.

Miner's Notes
  1. Pick a recipe from the cheat sheet and it drops straight onto the workbench.
  2. Or experiment. Put any materials on the grid and the "Could become" list shows every recipe you're working toward.
  3. Click the glowing result to craft it. New ones count toward your discovery bar.
  4. Wondering what an item is for? Put it on the grid and press "What uses this?" to filter the whole cheat sheet.
